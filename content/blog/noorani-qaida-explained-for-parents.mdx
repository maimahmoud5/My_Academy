Noorani Qaida is a beginner primer that teaches Arabic letters, sounds and joining letters — the foundation before fluent Quran reading.

## Why parents choose it

Children (and adult beginners) get a structured path from zero Arabic to reading short words.

## What comes after Qaida

Usually Quran reading practice, then Tajweed rules applied while reading. See our Noorani Qaida and Tajweed class pages for how we teach live.
