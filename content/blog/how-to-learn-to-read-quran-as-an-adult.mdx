It is never too late to learn. Adult beginners often start with the Arabic letters or Noorani Qaida — and that is completely normal.

## Expect a gentle start

You do not need any Arabic before lesson one. Classes are explained in English.

## Protect your schedule

Book a weekly slot you can keep. Consistency beats intensity.

## Ask for adult pacing

Tell your teacher you are an adult beginner so examples and pace fit you — not a child’s classroom.
