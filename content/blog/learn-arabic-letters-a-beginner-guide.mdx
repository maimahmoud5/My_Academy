Arabic has 28 letters. Many change shape depending on where they sit in a word. That sounds hard — but with live correction it becomes manageable.

## Learn sounds with a teacher

Recordings help, but a teacher hearing you aloud catches mistakes early.

## Letters before vocabulary

For Quran goals, letter recognition and joining often come before conversational Arabic. Our Arabic and Noorani Qaida classes can be combined depending on your goal.
