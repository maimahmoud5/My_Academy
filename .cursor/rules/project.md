# Noor Quran Academy — project rules

- English-only site. Beginners with no Arabic must feel welcome.
- Never invent credentials, prices, reviews, ratings, or student counts.
- Use `[TODO: ...]` for missing facts; list them in README.
- All editable content lives in `src/config/` and `src/data/`.
- Primary CTA: Book Free Trial. Secondary: WhatsApp.
- Do not load GA4 / Meta Pixel before cookie consent.
- Country pages stay `noindex` until `published: true` in `data/countries.ts`.
- Blog posts with `draft: true` must not appear in listings or sitemap.
- Arabic snippets: `lang="ar" dir="rtl"` and Amiri/Noto Naskh font.
- No heavy UI libraries; Lucide icons only.
- Image slots: `/public/images/placeholder-*.jpg` — no fake people photos.
